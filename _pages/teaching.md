## Introduction to Modeling in Earth Sciences

**Lead instructor, 2014**  
Second-year undergraduate course introducing the modeling process, from identifying essential physical processes and formulating a mathematical model to solving it and interpreting its assumptions and limitations.

**Teaching assistant, 2012–2013 and 2015**  
Led recitations, evaluated student work, and provided individual guidance in mathematical modeling and problem-solving.

## Dynamical Meteorology

**Teaching assistant, 2013**  
Supported instruction and evaluated student work in atmospheric dynamics and the mathematical description of rotating, stratified flows.

## Physics and mathematics tutoring

**Tutor, 2011–2016**  
Provided individual and small-group support for first-year undergraduate students through the Institute of Earth Sciences at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem.

## Research mentoring

**Co-mentor, three undergraduate research projects**  
Supported students' day-to-day research and development as independent researchers. One project contributed to a peer-reviewed publication, and all three students subsequently pursued graduate study in computer science, atmospheric science, and applied mathematics.
