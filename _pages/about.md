<div class="site-introduction">
  <p class="research-identity">Organized dynamics in the atmosphere and climate system</p>

  <p class="intro-lead">I study how organized dynamical structures emerge, evolve, and shape variability in the atmosphere and climate system. My research combines theory, numerical modeling, observations, and data-driven methods to identify these structures, understand the mechanisms that sustain them, and determine how they influence transport, predictability, and climate change.</p>

  <p>Across these problems, I use model hierarchies and physically constrained analysis to distinguish robust dynamics from artifacts of approximation, sampling, and random variability.</p>
