  <p class="intro-lead">I study atmospheric and climate dynamics by turning implicit physical and statistical assumptions into quantitative questions. My research examines when familiar descriptions of waves, variability, turbulence, and model behavior are physically justified, where they break down, and what dynamics are missed when they do. I develop theories, null models, and numerical tests that distinguish robust physical structure from the effects of approximation, random variability, and model bias.</p>

  <p>Through this work, I investigate how organized structures emerge, evolve, and shape climate variability, aiming to improve how climate dynamics are represented in physics-based and data-driven models.</p>
