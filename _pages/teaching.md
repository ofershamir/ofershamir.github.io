## Introduction to Modeling in Earth Sciences
{: .teaching-course }

Second-year undergraduate course introducing the modeling process, from identifying essential physical processes and formulating a mathematical model to solving it and interpreting its assumptions and limitations.

**Lead instructor**
Designed and delivered course instruction.

**Teaching assistant**
Led recitations, evaluated student work, and provided individual guidance in mathematical modeling and problem-solving.

## Dynamical Meteorology
{: .teaching-course }

Undergraduate course introducing the fundamental dynamics of the atmosphere, with emphasis on the effects of rotation, stratification, and balanced flow.

**Teaching assistant**
Supported instruction and evaluated student work in atmospheric dynamics and the mathematical description of rotating, stratified flows.

## Physics and mathematics tutoring
{: .teaching-course }

**Tutor**
Provided individual and small-group support for first-year undergraduate students.
